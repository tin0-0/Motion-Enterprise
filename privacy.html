Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Motion Enterprise Finance & Trade (“we”, “us”) provides short-term loans and buys and sells electronics and appliances. This policy explains how we handle personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us when you contact us on WhatsApp or by email. Depending on your enquiry, this may include your name, contact details, the amount and purpose of a loan request, and details or photos of items you want to buy or sell. We may ask for identity and income information when assessing a loan application.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, assess and administer loan applications, and complete buying or selling transactions. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We only share your information where it is needed to provide the service you asked for, or where the law requires us to. WhatsApp and email are third-party services with their own privacy practices.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access, and we keep it only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected or as the law requires.

Your rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email us at motionenterprisefnt@gmail.com and we will respond as soon as reasonably possible. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
